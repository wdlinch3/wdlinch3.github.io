Minicourses

Local snapshots of public guided-study packets and source materials for compact mathematical and physical minicourses.

These materials are copied from the public minicourses release repository so the student-facing artifacts can be read from this site. The collection is uneven by design: some minicourses are polished PDF packets, some are rendered web notes, and some are source notebooks for ongoing guided work.

Groups

A mechanics-oriented introduction to rotations, vectors, covectors, index notation, and the matrix language behind rotation groups. The packet introduces 3D linear algebra and rotations, then extends the same ideas toward \(SO(2)\), \(SO(3)\), commutativity, dimensions of \(SO(n)\), and standard matrix groups such as \(GL\), \(SL\), and \(O\).

Linear Algebra

A Research Club packet on orthogonal functions and the bridge from finite-dimensional linear algebra to analysis. It begins with eigenvectors, adjoints, self-adjoint operators, and Gram-Schmidt, then moves into function spaces, orthogonal polynomials, Sturm-Liouville problems, Fourier series, spherical symmetry, and complex Hilbert spaces.

Quantum Mechanics to Electromagnetism

A notebook-style derivation that starts from the irrelevance of an overall quantum phase and asks what changes when the phase is allowed to vary over spacetime. The notes introduce local rephasing, gauge covariance, the vector potential, the Aharonov-Bohm experiment, the magnetic field, the Faraday-Maxwell tensor, Stokes' theorem in differential-form language, charge and current, the spacetime metric, the Hodge dual, and Maxwell's equations.

The release provides this minicourse as a Jupyter notebook; local HTML and PDF renders are included here for easier reading.

Relativity

Two relativity packets are available. The electromagnetic-forms packet rewrites Maxwell theory from integral and vector-calculus forms into differential forms, the Faraday tensor, the compact Maxwell equations, and the Maxwell action with gauge invariance. The general-relativity packet moves from Newtonian unease about action at a distance to metrics as dynamical fields, Lorentz-covariant Maxwell equations, spacetime geometry, and the coordinate-independent structures needed for general relativity.

Renormalization Packet

A guided derivation of renormalization in a solvable quantum-mechanical model: a nonrelativistic particle in two spatial dimensions moving in an attractive delta-function potential. The packet is explicit that it is not passive introductory reading; the problems lead students through Bessel functions, Green functions, the logarithmic divergence, short-distance regularization, the running coupling, the beta function, dimensional transmutation, asymptotic freedom, and the scale anomaly.

Time Translations

A rendered web packet that develops time evolution by analogy with spatial translations. Starting from momentum as the generator of translations, it asks for the generator of time translations, identifies the role of energy, derives Hamilton's canonical equations, introduces Poisson brackets and conservation of the Hamiltonian, and then turns toward Hamiltonian quantization.
