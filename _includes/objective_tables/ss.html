AA: Solar System

For 2026–2027, the Geometry and Celestial Sphere objectives have been aligned with the current Geometry and Celestial Sphere I–II packets. Students are also responsible for assigned textbook material and classroom instruction, including Chapter 1; the existing requirements for planetary motion, lunar phases, tidal locking, eclipses, and precession/nutation are retained for separate instructor review of coverage. The Kepler objectives have also been reviewed against the current Kepler I packet; other later sections retain their previous wording pending review. Shared geometry material remains coded as aa.x.* because it belongs to the common AA:X strand even when it is used early in AA:SS.

Kepler’s Laws

Assessment scope (2026–2027): Related objectives remain grouped by topic. Unit 2 covers Kepler I geometry and representations and the Third Law applications developed with Dimensional Analysis and Astrodynamics I. Unit 2 also includes the qualitative equal-areas rule and orbital-speed variation in the assigned textbook sections. The follow-up Unit 3 develops the angular-momentum and energy explanations of orbital motion. Unit-specific assessment lists will identify the VLOs, or components of broader VLOs, being tested. Distinctions among anomalies and anomaly-conversion or Kepler-equation derivations are enrichment and will not be tested.
