The new school

1983–1988. Drum machines, beatboxing, sampling, and a more forceful vocal attack reorganize the relation among DJ, producer, and MC. Regional and transatlantic variants are already audible inside the supposedly New York-centered change.

Guiding question: How do harder drums, shorter phrases, and sparse space alter an MC’s authority?
