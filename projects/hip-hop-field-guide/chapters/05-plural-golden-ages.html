Plural golden ages

1988–1992. There is no single golden-age sound. Dense sample collage, Native Tongues play, gangsta reportage, formal lyricism, political pedagogy, and rapidly differentiating regional systems coexist and argue with one another.

Guiding question: What becomes possible when sampling, politics, narrative, virtuosity, and regional identity all intensify at once?
