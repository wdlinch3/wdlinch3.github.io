The South, mixtapes, and producer power

2001–2006. Crunk, early trap consolidation, Cash Money and No Limit afterlives, mixtapes, chopped-and-screwed listening, the Neptunes, Timbaland’s influence, and new global neighbors complicate any story of one national mainstream.

Guiding question: How do producer signatures and informal circulation routes move regional sounds toward the center?
