Before the name

1968–1973. Spoken-word militancy, Jamaican deejaying, funk drumming, break records, comedy routines, and vivid street narration all enter the prehistory. Treat these as tributaries and resources, not a mechanical hunt for one first rap record.

Guiding question: Which older practices became usable raw material without thereby becoming ‘hip-hop before hip-hop’?
