Regional systems

1992–1996. Local radio, labels, studios, crews, dance practices, and producer systems make Atlanta, Houston, Memphis, New Orleans, Chicago, New York, Los Angeles, France, Brazil, South Africa, and other scenes sound differently organized.

Guiding question: What does geography explain when it means infrastructure rather than a coastal feud?

081 — Dr. Dre featuring Snoop Doggy Dogg — “Nuthin’ but a ‘G’ Thang”

1992 · Los Angeles / Long Beach · G-funk · producer-auteur · flow

Play the SoundCloud recording · playlist-table entry

Why it is here. g-funk synthesis and producer-centered album world.

Listen for. Listen for where accents land against the beat, how phrases cross bar lines, and how vocal attack creates authority; then compare that with the relation between drum programming, low-end space, persona, and the record’s account of danger or street life.

Specified recording. original The Chronic album version.

Why the cliché survives. The canonical G-funk exhibit survives because its relaxed surface makes a major reorganization of bass, synthesis, swing, and regional center unusually easy to hear.
