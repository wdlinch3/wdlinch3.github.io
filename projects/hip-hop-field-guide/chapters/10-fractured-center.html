A newly fractured center

2011–2016. The streaming era does not erase place, but it changes how quickly forms migrate. Drill, trap, cloud aesthetics, industrial experiments, and sustained political lyricism occupy overlapping markets without converging on one grammar.

Guiding question: Can there still be one center when drill, mature trap, cloud rap, experimental albums, and global scenes circulate together?
