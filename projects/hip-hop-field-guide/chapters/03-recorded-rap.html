Recorded rap and electro

1979–1984. Early rap records move among disco-band accompaniment, party address, political reporting, studio collage, and electro futurism. Women and international adapters appear immediately; the history never belonged to one sonic template.

Guiding question: Which parts of live hip-hop could a record capture, and which parts did the record business change?

Why the cliché survives. This is a survey cliché because histories repeatedly use the first giant rap hit as their opening gate. It stays because the tension between a studio band, an adapted live routine, authorship, and commercial scale is itself indispensable.
