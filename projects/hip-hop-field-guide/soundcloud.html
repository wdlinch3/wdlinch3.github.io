SoundCloud listening edition

The guide contains 220 researched positions. 198 exact or explicitly qualified recordings are currently resolved and verified on SoundCloud; 22 exact recordings remain in the guide but are omitted from the playlist rather than silently replaced.

The account playlist is the intended one-click implementation. Until its final account write is complete, the table below is also a fully playable ordered edition: open links in sequence or use it to jump back and forth between sound and explanation.

TipSoundCloud subscription

Ninety entries are in SoundCloud’s subscriber catalog; 108 are open or ad-supported. Tom’s subscription should make the full 198-track route playable. Platform availability can still change after verification.
