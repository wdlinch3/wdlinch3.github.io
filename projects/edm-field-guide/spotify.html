Listen on Spotify

The Spotify implementation is built from the researched manifest rather than generated from a descriptive prompt. The track-order-verified Spotify edition contains the 146 requested recordings currently playable in the account’s United States catalog, in their original manifest-relative order.

NoteWhat 146 + 12 means

The guide discusses 158 recordings. Spotify has playable matches for 146 of those exact requests. The remaining 12 are guide selections for which the requested recording or mix is not currently playable; they are not twelve substitutes or alternate versions in the playlist. They remain in the guide and appear in the catalog-gap table below.

Verification rules

  • The track title, credited artist, requested mix or version, duration, and release context must agree.
  • A merely similar recording does not count as an exact match.
  • Market relinking, alternate reissues, and unavailable originals remain explicit decisions.
  • The shared account’s listening history is not evidence about William’s preferences.
  • An empty or partially populated playlist shell is never reported as complete.
