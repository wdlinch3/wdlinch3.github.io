Listen on Spotify

The Spotify implementation is built from the researched manifest rather than generated from a descriptive prompt. The track-order-verified Spotify edition contains the 146 requested recordings currently playable in the account’s United States catalog, in their original manifest-relative order. The 12 unavailable exact versions remain in the 158-track curriculum and are explicitly marked in their guide entries and in the catalog-gap table below.

Verification rules

  • The track title, credited artist, requested mix or version, duration, and release context must agree.
  • A merely similar recording does not count as an exact match.
  • Market relinking, alternate reissues, and unavailable originals remain explicit decisions.
  • The shared account’s listening history is not evidence about William’s preferences.
  • An empty or partially populated playlist shell is never reported as complete.

Current literal status: first-party PKCE authorization and the full catalog-resolution pass are complete. No neighboring edit, remix, re-recording, or DJ-mix excerpt was substituted for the 12 omitted versions.
